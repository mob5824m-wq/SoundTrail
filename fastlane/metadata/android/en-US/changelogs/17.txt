Soundtrail now contains no proprietary Google libraries, so it can be built and distributed as free software (for example by F-Droid).
- YouTube connects with your own Desktop OAuth client ID in the browser. The Play services sign-in is removed.
- Motion for automations comes from GPS speed. The optional Google activity recognition is removed.
- YouTube audio extraction is removed: Soundtrail no longer downloads YouTube audio, and YouTube songs open in the YouTube Music app.
