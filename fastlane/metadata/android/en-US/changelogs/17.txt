Soundtrail now contains no proprietary Google libraries, so it can be built and distributed as free software (for example by F-Droid).
- YouTube connects with your own Desktop OAuth client ID in the browser. The Google Play services sign-in is removed.
- Motion for automations comes from GPS speed. The optional Google activity recognition is removed.
- The optional YouTube audio extraction is removed. Soundtrail no longer downloads YouTube audio, and the sync server no longer has a YouTube endpoint. YouTube songs open in the YouTube Music app.
